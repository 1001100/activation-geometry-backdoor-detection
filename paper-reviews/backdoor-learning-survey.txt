Title: Backdoor Learning: A Survey

Author(s): Yiming Li et al.

Link: arXiv.2007.08745

Core Idea: Claimed first of kind comprehensive literature review on the topic of backdoors. This includes definitions, syntax, and equations, as well as all attacks and defenses published. Directly relevant to project and the best guide to exploring literature  in field currently

Method: N/A

Most widely adopted attacks consist of poisoning training samples to encode backdoor functionality. 

Empirical Backdoor Defense: Defenses proposed based on the analysis or understanding of the weakness of the attack have decent performance in practice; however, their effectiveness have no theoretical guarantee and may probably be bypassed by some adaptive attacks

Certified Backdoor Defence: theoretically guaranteed under certain assumptions, whereas its performance is generally weaker than that of empirical defenses in practice since those assumptions are usually unsatisfied

Risk Scenarios: Adopt third party datasets, third party platforms, third party models
- Each present different levels of risk to user, opportunity for defense, and control to attacker

A unified framework of poisoning-based attacks
- equations and paragraphs quantifying risk for attacks (reference briefly toward attack stealthiness or complexity)

Evaluation metrics: Benign accuracy, attack success rate
